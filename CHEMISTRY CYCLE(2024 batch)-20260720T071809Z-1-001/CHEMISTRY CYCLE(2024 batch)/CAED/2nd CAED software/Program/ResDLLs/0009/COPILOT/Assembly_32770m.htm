SmartStep helps you position parts in the assembly by prompting for more relationships until the the part is fully positioned. But you can choose other commands while the new part's position is not completely defined.

Flash Fit is the quickest and easiest way to mate and align new components in the assembly. You can also choose more complex relationships, such as Tangent, Cam, and Angle, by selecting them from the list.

Each relationship has its own set of options that help you position the part in the assembly.

Follow the prompts for an explanation on what to do.
