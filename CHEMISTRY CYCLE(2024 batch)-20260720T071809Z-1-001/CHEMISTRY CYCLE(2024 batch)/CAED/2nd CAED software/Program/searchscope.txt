\\ This document can contain the following valid entries so that system knows where to search...
\\
\\ 1. A valid URL to a Document Library 
\\    EXAMPLE: http://hsvnt261/SolidEdge
\\
\\ 2. A valid URL to a Site. in this case, Insight will use all 
\\    valid Document Libraries in the Site. Validity includes presence of  
\\    Insight mandatory properties such as SELinkData.
\\    
\\    EXAMPLE: http://hsvnt261 
\\    Document Libraries in the Site - such as http://hsvnt261/SolidEdge and
\\    http://hsvnt261/Doclib will be used by Insight.
\\    
\\ 3. You can exclude a particular Document Library from being used by Insight,  by 
\\    including the keyword "Exception!".
\\
\\    EXAMPLE: Exception!http://hsvnt261/SolidEdge 
\\
\\  NOTE: All lines starting with "\\" are ignored
